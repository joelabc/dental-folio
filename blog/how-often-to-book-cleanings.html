How often should you really book dental cleanings?

The six-month guideline is a good default, but not every patient needs the same schedule. Gum health, home care, restorations, and lifestyle factors all affect the ideal interval.

March 18, 2026 5 min read By Dr. Maya Chen

The standard interval for most adults

Many adults benefit from professional hygiene visits every six months because plaque and tartar buildup can accumulate even with good brushing and flossing habits. This cadence also creates a predictable opportunity to spot small issues before they become expensive ones.

When more frequent visits make sense

Patients with active gum inflammation, heavy tartar buildup, orthodontic appliances, dry mouth, or a history of rapid decay may need three- or four-month recalls instead. The same can be true during pregnancy or after certain restorative treatments.

Why customized recall schedules matter

Preventive care is most effective when the timing matches the patient's risk level. A customized recall schedule is not upselling when it is based on visible clinical need; it is a way to keep small problems from progressing.

Quick summary

  • Six months is a strong default for many adults.
  • Higher-risk patients may need shorter intervals.
  • Recall frequency should follow clinical need, not habit alone.
